    Privacy

    FMHY Similar has no server, no account and no analytics. Everything it knows lives in your browser's extension storage and never leaves your device.

    What is stored

    • Your settings — the options on this page (synced by your browser profile, if you have sync on).
    • Your favorites — only resources you explicitly star: name, category, description and links.
    • The resource database — the public FMHY wiki turned into structured JSON, stored locally.
    • Recently viewed — up to 50 resources you opened from a Find Similar card. Optional, and clearable.

    What is never collected

    • No browsing history. The extension does not have, and does not request, the history or tabs permission.
    • No FMHY URLs are sent anywhere. Resource matching happens entirely against the local database.
    • No telemetry, no analytics, no identifiers, no fingerprinting.
    • No scripts are injected into third-party resource websites — the content script only runs on fmhy.net.

    Network activity

    The only network requests this extension ever makes are the dataset downloads you trigger with Update database (or the optional daily check above). Those requests go to FMHY's own published markdown, contain no information about you, and happen at most once per day.
